## Data-source split (critical for adapter design)

1. **yahoo+marketstack (both cover):** all `.TW`, `.HK`, `.T`, `.KS`, and US ADRs (VFS, PDD, CPNG, ETERNAL.NS). "Just work."
2. **yahoo-only (Marketstack does NOT cover Vietnam HOSE):** ALL `.VN` HOSE names (FPT, CMG, CTR, MWG, VTP, FRT, DGW, SGT, ELC, ITD, PET). **Consequence:** if the price feed is Marketstack-primary, every VN row silently returns nothing — need a Yahoo fallback adapter for `.VN`, or these go stale. **Single most important integration note.**
3. **vietnam-native (vnstock/FireAnt/SSI or manual):** VGI on UPCoM — `VGI.VN` 404s on Yahoo. Needs a VN-native source or manual entry; drop/mark-manual if you don't want a second VN adapter.
